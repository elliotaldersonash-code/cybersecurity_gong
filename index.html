About me

I'm a student fascinated by the way systems break and the way they are defended. Cybersecurity is where my curiosity and my love for programming meet: every vulnerability is a puzzle, and every fix is a lesson.

I learn by building: small scanners, scripts and bots, then testing them in legal lab environments and CTF challenges. My goal is to become a skilled ethical hacker who protects people and shares what I learn along the way.
